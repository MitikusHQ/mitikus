# How to Digitize Your Consulting Firm Processes Step by Step

> Digitizing a consulting firm doesn't mean buying more software: it means eliminating real friction. Here's a concrete plan to do it without wasting time along the way.

## The problem nobody talks about out loud

Most consulting firms live a double life. On the outside, they advise their clients on efficiency, digital transformation, or change management. On the inside, they manage projects in spreadsheets, send contracts by email for clients to print and sign by hand, and waste hours figuring out which version of a document is the final one.

It's not a judgment: it's the reality of many consulting firms, large and small. The problem isn't a lack of will, but rather that digitizing seems like an enormous and vague project. Where do you start? What tools do you need? How do you convince the team?

The good news is that digitizing your processes doesn't require a six-month transformation or an enterprise-level budget. It requires order, priority, and advancing in phases.

## Phase 1: Map your processes before touching anything

The most common mistake is jumping into installing tools without understanding what processes actually exist. Before digitizing anything, spend two hours listing everything your consulting firm does repeatedly:

- Prospecting and lead follow-up
- New client onboarding
- Contract and proposal creation and signing
- Internal task and project management
- Invoice issuance and payment follow-up
- Team time tracking and timesheets
- Report and deliverable generation

For each process, note three things: who does it, how much time it takes, and where information gets lost. That exercise alone will tell you what to digitize first.

**Priority criterion:** start with the processes that create the most friction with clients or cost you the most administrative time. Usually these are contracts, billing, and project tracking.

## Phase 2: Digitize in blocks, not all at once

Once you have the map, work in functional blocks. Trying to digitize everything in parallel guarantees chaos.

**Block 1 — Documentation and contracts**
Replace PDF exchanges by email with a system where contracts are generated from templates, sent to the client, and signed digitally without leaving the platform. This eliminates duplicate versions, accelerates closing, and creates legal traceability. In MITIKUS, for example, you can manage contracts with integrated digital signatures without needing external tools.

**Block 2 — Billing and clients**
Connect your client database with invoice generation. If every time you invoice you have to manually copy data, you're wasting time. A basic CRM linked to billing eliminates that duplicate work and reduces errors.

**Block 3 — Project and task management**
Each consulting project has phases, deliverables, and responsible parties. If that lives in email or a shared spreadsheet, information gets scattered. Move management to a task system where each team member sees what they need to do, when it's due, and the overall project status.

**Block 4 — Timesheets and time tracking**
If you bill by the hour or need to justify effort to the client, digital time tracking isn't optional. A reliable record of hours also helps you better monetize each project.

## Phase 3: Consolidate and automate

Once the blocks work separately, the next step is to reduce the manual work remaining between them. Some concrete examples:

- **Automate onboarding:** when you sign a contract, automatically generate a welcome task and create the client file.
- **Create proposal templates:** don't draft each proposal from scratch. Start with a base structure and personalize only what's relevant.
- **Define workflows by project type:** if you do audits, implementations, or training, each type should have its own standard task flow.
- **Review metrics regularly:** average time per project, collection rate, contracts pending signature. Data only matters if you look at it.

The key isn't automating for the sake of automating, but eliminating repetitive decisions that steal your focus.
